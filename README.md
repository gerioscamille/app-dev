**My Favorite Series: Doctor Romantic**

**Doctor Romantic** is a *South Korean medical drama* that follows the life of a genius surgeon, **Boo Yong-joo**, also known as **Teacher Kim**.
He works at a small hospital called *Doldam Hospital*, where he mentors young doctors and teaches them the true meaning of being a doctor — saving lives without prejudice or greed.
---
**Main Characters**
- Teacher Kim (Han Suk-kyu) — The brilliant and compassionate surgeon
- Kang Dong-joo (Yoo Yeon-seok) — A talented young doctor with a rough past
- Yoon Seo-jung (Seo Hyun-jin) — A passionate surgeon striving for redemption

---
**What I Like About It**
1. Inspiring lessons about integrity and compassion in medicine
2. Emotional storytelling and strong character development
3. Realistic hospital scenes and powerful life lessons
